# SLO and SLI Standard

## SLO domains

- reliability
- quality
- compliance and safety
- cost sustainability

## Example SLIs

- p95 retrieval latency
- citation audit pass percentage
- retrieval drift on gold queries
- access-control error rate
- schema and shape validation pass rates
- cost per retrieval or grounded answer event

## Rule

Compliance and safety SLOs are near-zero tolerance where leakage or unauthorized access is possible.
