# Research Signal Ingestion Policy

Research signals are evidence, not authority.

Phase 2 supports local-only research and discovery objects:

- existing `schemas/discovery-signal.schema.json`;
- `schemas/research/research-request-object.schema.json`;
- `schemas/research/research-brief-object.schema.json`;
- `schemas/research/incident-analogy-record.schema.json`.

Research may inform:

- assumption watch records;
- opportunity objects;
- opportunity scorecards;
- harness plans;
- Codex task packets;
- human decision packets.

Research must not:

- mutate canonical ontology truth;
- create route IDs or event classes;
- restore green authority;
- execute code or patches;
- perform live web crawling;
- call live external APIs;
- write externally;
- become production research automation.

Live research automation requires a separate approved policy and implementation path.
