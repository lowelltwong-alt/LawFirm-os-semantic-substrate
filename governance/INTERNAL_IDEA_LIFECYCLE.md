# Internal Idea Lifecycle

Internal ideas are local proposal objects. They are not execution authority.

Allowed lifecycle states:

- draft;
- under review;
- parked;
- killed;
- resurrection candidate;
- converted to opportunity.

Ideas may be generated by humans or agents, red-teamed, parked, killed, combined, or converted into opportunity objects.

Dead or parked ideas may be resurrected only when new evidence, research, incidents, or operating signals materially change the assumptions that caused the idea to be killed or parked.

Idea resurrection remains proposal-only until a human or approved governed lane authorizes the next step.
