# Attribution Scope Policy

Author: Lowell T. Wong

## Purpose

This repository is authored and owned by Lowell T. Wong.

Attribution should reflect:
- Lowell T. Wong as primary and sole author of the system
- External influences may be acknowledged conceptually but not treated as co-authorship unless explicitly defined

## Specific Rule

Only AIRCA-related concepts may include shared conceptual credit where explicitly appropriate.

All other:
- ontology design
- fractal address system
- governance layers
- validation systems
- enterprise learning model

are attributed solely to Lowell T. Wong.

## Enforcement

Future contributions (human or AI) should:
- not introduce external names as co-authors
- not imply shared ownership of the system
- preserve attribution clarity across all artifacts

## Rationale

Clear attribution is necessary for:
- intellectual ownership
- system coherence
- governance integrity

This policy should be treated as authoritative.
