# Slice 02 — Litify Exception to Executive Brief

## Goal

Show how a Litify or adjacent operational issue becomes a governed exception event, a pressure vector, an opportunity, and a decision-ready executive brief.

## Flow

1. Exception event is captured with scope and provenance.
2. Pressure vector normalizes the repeated operational signal.
3. Opportunity object ranks the intervention.
4. Validation and ownership are made explicit.
5. Executive brief renders the decision packet.

## Success condition

A leader can see what changed, why it matters, who owns it, and what evidence supports the recommendation.
