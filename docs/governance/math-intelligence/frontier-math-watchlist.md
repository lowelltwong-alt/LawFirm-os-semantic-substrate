# Frontier Math Watchlist

This watchlist records candidate signals and review triggers. It does not certify theorem truth, production readiness, or immediate implementation value.

## Watch categories

### Formal methods and proof systems

- Watch item: proof assistants, verified compilation, proof-carrying workflows
  - Why it may matter: stronger contract verification and audit reasoning
  - Current status: watch
  - Next review trigger: official publication, formalized proof artifact, or benchmark with reproducible materials

### Optimization and control

- Watch item: constrained optimization, robust control, multi-objective planning
  - Why it may matter: governed planning, route selection, and resource tradeoff analysis
  - Current status: watch
  - Next review trigger: architecturally relevant benchmark or scoped prototype recommendation

### Probabilistic reasoning and causality

- Watch item: Bayesian updating, uncertainty quantification, causal discovery methods
  - Why it may matter: uncertainty-aware retrieval, triage, and evidence handling
  - Current status: watch
  - Next review trigger: peer-reviewed synthesis or benchmark result with clear boundary conditions

### Graph, topology, and geometry

- Watch item: graph embeddings, spectral methods, topological summaries
  - Why it may matter: ontology structure, retrieval surfaces, and relationship reasoning
  - Current status: watch
  - Next review trigger: formalized method with architecturally relevant impact analysis

### Cryptography and privacy-preserving computation

- Watch item: zero-knowledge methods, secure multiparty computation, cryptographic proofs
  - Why it may matter: transcript governance, retention boundaries, and audit controls
  - Current status: watch
  - Next review trigger: official source update or benchmark tied to governed privacy constraints

## Source-status bands

- `rumor`: unverified public signal; no truth claim
- `preprint_claim`: public claim with preliminary materials; no verification implied
- `formalized_proof`: proof artifact exists in a formal system
- `peer_reviewed`: externally reviewed publication exists
- `replicated`: independent reproduction exists
- `benchmark_result`: measurable result exists under named conditions
- `disputed`: public disagreement or unresolved challenge exists
