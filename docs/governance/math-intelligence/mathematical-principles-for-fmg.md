# Mathematical Principles for Law Firm

These principles summarize why selected math areas may matter to Law Firm systems and governance. They do not claim that AI or math will solve all math, and they do not claim current production use unless separately established elsewhere.

## Principles by domain

- Linear algebra
  - Matters for embeddings, transformations, ranking surfaces, and structured representations.
- Probability and statistics
  - Matters for uncertainty description, evaluation, and evidence-quality interpretation.
- Bayesian inference
  - Matters for explicit prior assumptions, posterior updates, and uncertainty-aware review.
- Optimization
  - Matters for constrained planning, tradeoff analysis, and bounded resource allocation.
- Information theory
  - Matters for signal value, compression, redundancy, and evidence efficiency.
- Graph theory and spectral graph theory
  - Matters for ontology structure, relationship analysis, and retrieval over connected knowledge.
- Logic and proof theory
  - Matters for formal validity, rule consistency, and auditable reasoning chains.
- Model theory
  - Matters for interpreting formal structures and checking meaning across representations.
- Category theory
  - Matters for compositional structure, interfaces, and safe abstraction boundaries.
- Topology and geometry
  - Matters for neighborhood structure, similarity, and geometric reasoning about spaces.
- Dynamical systems
  - Matters for stability, feedback loops, and evolving learning or control surfaces.
- Control theory
  - Matters for governed feedback, safe adjustment, and bounded intervention logic.
- Game theory
  - Matters for multi-agent coordination, incentives, and adversarial or strategic settings.
- Numerical analysis
  - Matters for approximation quality, stability, and computational reliability.
- Measure theory and analysis
  - Matters for rigorous probability foundations, continuity, limits, and approximation behavior.
- Complexity theory
  - Matters for tractability, infeasibility, and truthful claims about what can scale.
- Cryptography
  - Matters for audit integrity, transcript governance, retention boundaries, and secure verification.
- Causal inference
  - Matters for distinguishing correlation from intervention-relevant structure in governed learning.

## Governed recursive improvement

Mathematical advances can become governed-learning inputs for governed recursive improvement. They do not grant direct self-modification authority.

Use the existing path:

`candidate signal -> assessment -> recommendation -> adaptation-proposal recommendation if reviewed -> promotion-decision only through existing authority path`
