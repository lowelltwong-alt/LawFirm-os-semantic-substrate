# Proof Status Taxonomy

Use these labels to describe what kind of support exists for a math-intelligence item.

## Taxonomy

- `rumor`
  - Meaning: An unverified public claim or mention.
  - Allowed inference: The topic may be worth watching.
  - Does not justify: truth claims, roadmap change, prototype claims, or canonical change.

- `preprint_claim`
  - Meaning: A public claim accompanied by preliminary paper, notes, or release materials.
  - Allowed inference: The topic may deserve summary or watch status with explicit uncertainty.
  - Does not justify: verified truth, production claims, or automatic implementation.

- `formalized_proof`
  - Meaning: A proof artifact exists in a formal proof system or equivalent rigorous representation.
  - Allowed inference: Stronger support for correctness review within the declared scope.
  - Does not justify: architectural fit, production value, or automatic canon mutation.

- `peer_reviewed`
  - Meaning: A reviewed publication or accepted scholarly artifact exists.
  - Allowed inference: Higher confidence that the claim merits governed assessment.
  - Does not justify: direct implementation, business-value claims, or production readiness.

- `replicated`
  - Meaning: Independent reproduction or validation exists under documented conditions.
  - Allowed inference: Stronger confidence in bounded empirical or formal claims.
  - Does not justify: universal applicability or automatic Law Firm adoption.

- `benchmark_result`
  - Meaning: Measured performance or comparison result exists under stated benchmark conditions.
  - Allowed inference: Candidate evidence for scoped impact analysis.
  - Does not justify: theorem truth, generalized production benefit, or unsupported extrapolation.

- `disputed`
  - Meaning: Significant unresolved challenge, contradiction, or criticism exists.
  - Allowed inference: Caution, watch status, or scoped follow-up only.
  - Does not justify: promotion, implementation claims, or settled truth language.

- `architecturally_relevant`
  - Meaning: The item appears relevant to one or more Law Firm surfaces after review.
  - Allowed inference: It may justify roadmap or prototype discussion.
  - Does not justify: direct change authority or correctness-by-relevance.

- `promotion_candidate`
  - Meaning: The item may warrant later governed promotion discussion after review and proper authority checks.
  - Allowed inference: Consider an adaptation-proposal recommendation.
  - Does not justify: direct promotion decision or canon mutation.
