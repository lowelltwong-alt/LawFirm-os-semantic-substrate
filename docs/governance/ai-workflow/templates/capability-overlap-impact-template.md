# Capability Overlap Impact Template

Use for overlap, derivative, orchestration, and impact review across capability cards or families.

## Route
- route: `agent_skill_capability_graph`
- mode: Plan/Edit

## Required sections

- Compared capability cards or families
- Overlap or derivative classification
- Canonical versus derived evidence used
- Orchestrator impact assessment
- Notification and subscription impact
- Merge, keep-separate, or alias recommendation
- Audit and governed-learning implications
- Whether an `adaptation-proposal` recommendation is warranted

## Evidence reminder

Derived overlap reports, similarity neighborhoods, embeddings, or GraphRAG summaries may support review, but they may not settle canonical merge or promotion decisions by themselves.

## Forbidden

- automatic merges
- automatic runtime changes
- generated artifacts treated as canonical truth
- raw transcript capture
- autonomous repo rewriting
