# ADR-0004 Legal Ontology Watchlist

## Status
Accepted

## Decision

The following remain on a watchlist for later review:

- LKIF Core
- LegalRuleML
- Akoma Ntoso

## Why Watchlist Later

These may become useful for deeper legal-domain interoperability, but they are premature for the current pilot.

## Why Not Adopt Now

The current proof of concept is centered on Law Firm operating governance for intake, conflicts, and AI-use decisions. Adopting these standards now would risk shifting the design toward legal-document formalism before the substrate-native operating layer is stable.

## Meaning Of Watchlist

Watchlist later does not mean rejected.
It means not core yet.

## Review Trigger

Revisit when the pilot expands into richer legal-rule modeling, structured legal-document exchange, or repeated requests for external legal ontology interoperability.
